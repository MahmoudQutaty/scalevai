Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How does ScaleVAI eliminate buddy-punching and ghost hours in factories and warehouses?

ScaleVAI connects directly to existing RTSP/IP camera feeds over turnstiles, security gates, and break room corridors. Our computer vision models verify actual face-to-badge ground-truth presence in real time. If a card is swiped without a matching physical person or if multiple entries occur on one badge, an instant anomaly flag is generated and synced with your payroll and WMS system.

Can ScaleVAI operate on our existing industrial CCTV cameras without replacing hardware?

Yes. ScaleVAI requires zero new proprietary turnstiles or sensor hardware. Our edge processors and cloud pipelines interface with standard ONVIF and RTSP IP security camera streams from Hikvision, Dahua, Axis, and Hanwha already mounted in your facility.

How does ScaleVAI accelerate truck loading dock turnaround times?

By automating license plate recognition (ALPR), container serial recognition, and AI-powered optical document extraction for bills of lading (BOL), delivery orders, and customs declarations. Drivers check in via automated kiosks or mobile QR codes, reducing gate dwell times from 45+ minutes to under 8 minutes.

Does ScaleVAI integrate with our ERP and Warehouse Management Systems (SAP, Oracle, Blue Yonder)?

Yes. ScaleVAI provides production-ready connectors and REST/webhook APIs for SAP S/4HANA, Oracle NetSuite, Blue Yonder, Manhattan Associates, Microsoft Dynamics 365, and custom WMS/TMS solutions to synchronize time and attendance, dock schedules, and dispatch milestones.
