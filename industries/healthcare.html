Frequently Asked Questions

How does ScaleVAI reduce insurance claim denials for UAE & GCC healthcare providers?

ScaleVAI acts as an intelligent pre-submission scrubber. Before claims reach insurance clearinghouses (such as eClaimLink for DHA, Shafafiya/Malafi for DoH, or NPHIES in Saudi Arabia), our AI audits every line item against specific payer policies, medical necessity criteria, prior-authorization numbers, and ICD-10-AM / CPT coding pairings. This catches 85%+ of rejection-prone errors before submission.

Can ScaleVAI integrate with our existing Hospital Information System (HIS / EHR)?

Yes. ScaleVAI integrates seamlessly with major healthcare platforms including Epic, Cerner Oracle Health, Intersystems TrakCare, Kareo, Meditech, and custom legacy HIS databases via HL7, FHIR, and secure REST APIs, requiring zero replacement of existing clinical infrastructure.

Is patient health data secure and compliant with UAE and regional health data laws?

Yes. ScaleVAI is fully compliant with UAE Federal Health Data Law (Law No. 2 of 2019), Dubai Health Authority (DHA) data privacy standards, Department of Health (DoH) Abu Dhabi policies, and international HIPAA standards. All sensitive patient identifiable data remains encrypted both in transit and at rest within local cloud regions.

How does the AI patient concierge manage bilingual appointments and clinical triage?

Our AI Patient Concierge operates 24/7 across WhatsApp and clinic web portals in native Khaleeji Arabic and English. It books appointments based on doctor specialty, checks insurance eligibility in real time, answers prep instructions for procedures, and sends automated reminder alerts that cut patient no-show rates by up to 45%.
