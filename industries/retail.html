Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How does ScaleVAI comply with UAE data privacy laws and shopper anonymity?

ScaleVAI operates in strict compliance with UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on Personal Data Protection and global GDPR regulations. Our computer vision algorithms process shopper motion on local edge devices, converting feeds into anonymous mathematical vectors (dwell coordinates and bounding boxes). No facial biometric identifiers, names, or video recordings of shoppers are ever stored or transmitted to external servers.

Can we deploy ScaleVAI using our existing store CCTV cameras?

Yes. ScaleVAI is camera-agnostic and connects seamlessly via standard RTSP and ONVIF video streams from your existing ceiling CCTV cameras (Hikvision, Dahua, Axis, Hanwha, Uniview). You do not need to replace your hardware or install expensive proprietary in-store sensors.

How does on-shelf stockout detection differ from traditional RFID or POS inventory?

Traditional POS systems tell you what has been sold, but cannot detect "phantom stockouts"—items recorded as present in the store's inventory system that are sitting stranded in the stockroom or misplaced on the wrong shelf. ScaleVAI's computer vision continuously monitors display fixtures and hangers in real time, alerting store staff to replenish empty shelf facings before a walking shopper abandons the purchase.

Does ScaleVAI integrate with enterprise ERPs like SAP Retail and Oracle Xstore?

Yes. ScaleVAI provides robust bi-directional APIs for SAP S/4HANA Retail, Oracle Retail / Xstore, Microsoft Dynamics 365 Commerce, and Shopify POS. This enables unified correlation between footfall traffic, staff hours, and POS cash register conversions.
