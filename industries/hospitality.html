Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does the WhatsApp AI Concierge handle guest requests and room charges?

The AI Concierge connects directly to your Property Management System (such as Oracle Opera Cloud, Infor HMS, or Protel). When an authenticated guest orders in-room dining, books a spa session, or requests a late checkout, the charge is automatically posted to their guest folio while dispatching a task ticket to the appropriate department.

Does ScaleVAI integrate with Oracle Opera Cloud and existing hotel PMS systems?

Yes. ScaleVAI provides certified bi-directional API integrations for Oracle Hospitality Opera (v5 and Opera Cloud), Protel, Infor HMS, and Amadeus Hospitality, ensuring real-time guest status, reservation lookups, and room maintenance synchronization.

Can computer vision track housekeeping shifts without intruding on guest privacy?

Absolutely. ScaleVAI strictly monitors back-of-house service corridors, staff lockers, linen dispatch rooms, and service elevators. We never place or process cameras in guest rooms, suites, or private spaces. All attendance analytics measure contractor agency hours and linen room staging strictly in operational service zones.

How does the 3D Virtual Showroom accelerate wedding and corporate MICE bookings?

ScaleVAI creates interactive, photorealistic digital twins of ballrooms, conference halls, and presidential suites. International event organizers and wedding planners can remotely customize table layouts, lighting themes, stage placements, and catering menus in real time, closing deals up to 3x faster without waiting for in-person site visits.
