Frequently Asked Questions

How does ScaleVAI accelerate off-plan property sales for UAE & GCC developers?

ScaleVAI deploys Immersive Showroom 3D digital twins that allow international and remote buyers to walk through units, customize finishes, evaluate views, and review floor plans directly in any web browser or via WhatsApp links without installing dedicated apps. This converts prospects faster and gives overseas investors the confidence to commit before visiting Dubai or the GCC.

Can ScaleVAI's Workforce Intelligence work with our existing site cameras and turnstiles?

Yes. ScaleVAI's Workforce Intelligence overlays directly onto existing RTSP/IP camera feeds at site gates, service elevators, and security posts. It uses computer vision to log verified ground-truth presence without requiring new biometric turnstile hardware, eliminating badge swapping and unverified contractor invoices.

How does the AI tenant concierge handle bilingual requests and Ejari renewals?

Our AI Tenant Concierge natively understands Khaleeji Arabic dialects and English. It connects directly to your property management systems to process maintenance requests with photo diagnostics, dispatch technicians, answer amenity questions, provide gate passes, and issue automated Ejari renewal reminders.

Can ScaleVAI integrate with our existing property management ERP (Yardi, MRI, SAP, Oracle)?

Yes. ScaleVAI integrates with major real estate platforms including Yardi Voyager, MRI Software, Salesforce Real Estate Cloud, and SAP through secure APIs and automated middleware, synchronizing tenant status, payment milestones, and contractor audit logs in real time.
