Immersive Showroom FAQ

How long does it take to build an immersive showroom?

Most experiences go live within a few weeks of capture, depending on the size of the space and how much guided storytelling is required.

Do buyers need to install an app or headset?

No. Immersive Showroom runs in a standard web browser on desktop and mobile, so prospects can explore instantly from a link.

Can it work for products as well as properties?

Yes. The same approach applies to premium products, showrooms, retail spaces, and event venues.

Can we embed it in our existing website and campaigns?

Yes. Experiences can be embedded in your site, shared as a direct link, or attached to sales and marketing campaigns.
