Virtual Experience FAQ

Do guests need special equipment to use Virtual Experience?

No. Guests can explore in a normal web browser on desktop or mobile without installing an app or using a headset.

Can it support event venues and hotels?

Yes. It is designed for hospitality venues, event spaces, premium rooms, showrooms, and other high-consideration spaces.

Can the tour be embedded on our website?

Yes. The experience can be embedded in your website, shared through a direct link, and used in sales and campaign journeys.
