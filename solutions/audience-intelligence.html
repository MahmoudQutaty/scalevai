How it works

From passive bystander to captivated customer in 200 milliseconds.

An end-to-end edge AI pipeline that detects, classifies, triggers, and benchmarks audience engagement with 100% on-device privacy protection.

Sense

Discreet optical sensors capture foot-traffic presence, viewer distance, and head orientation within an active 12-meter cone.

Analyze

Lightweight neural networks estimate anonymous demographics (age group, gender bracket) and measure exact gaze dwell duration.

Trigger

Sends instant rule-based trigger commands to your display CMS, dynamically serving the optimal ad creative or localized message.

Measure

Consolidates verified impressions, dwell analytics, and conversion lift into real-time operational dashboards.

Audience Intelligence FAQ

Is the system compliant with UAE privacy regulations?

Yes, absolutely. Audience Intelligence is built on a strict privacy-first architecture compliant with UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 (PDPL) and international GDPR guidelines. All optical computer vision calculations occur entirely on-device (at the edge). Video frames are processed in volatile memory in fractions of a second and immediately discarded. No photos, videos, facial identities, or personal data are ever stored or transmitted.

How does Audience Intelligence integrate with our existing screens and CMS?

The solution integrates via lightweight API triggers or local UDP/HTTP event packets compatible with industry-standard digital signage players including BrightSign, Samsung Tizen/MagicINFO, LG webOS Signage, Windows media players, and Android boxes. Your existing CMS retains full content scheduling authority while our AI provides real-time trigger overrides.

How quickly does the screen react when a new viewer approaches?

The entire pipeline - from optical detection through neural classification to media player content swap - executes in under 250 milliseconds. Transitions are smooth and natural, ensuring viewers experience relevant content the exact moment they look toward the display.

What sensors or hardware are required?

ScaleVAI provides compact, low-power edge AI optical sensor modules that mount discreetly above or below your existing screens, connecting via USB or Ethernet. Alternatively, if your displays are already equipped with embedded webcams or nearby CCTV feeds, our software can utilize those existing streams directly.
