People Assistant FAQ

How does People Assistant prevent employees from seeing confidential peer data?

People Assistant enforces strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). When an employee connects via Microsoft Teams, Slack, or WhatsApp, their identity is cryptographically linked to their verified employee profile in your HRIS. An employee can only query their own leave balances, payslips, or gratuity figures.

Is the UAE Labour Law calculation engine kept up to date?

Yes. The calculation engine is pre-built on Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021. It automatically computes gratuity based on 21 days basic salary per year of service for the first 5 years, and 30 days for each additional year, pro-rated for fractions of a year, while strictly adhering to statutory salary caps and contract stipulations.

Does it work natively in both Arabic and English?

Yes. People Assistant was fine-tuned specifically for the GCC workforce. It understands Modern Standard Arabic, Gulf (Emirati, Saudi, Kuwaiti) phrasing, Levantine and Egyptian dialects, as well as Arabizi (Latin alphabet representation of Arabic). It responds naturally in the language the employee initiates with.

Can it generate official documents like bank salary certificates?

Yes. For approved document workflows (such as embassy visa letters, salary certificates for bank loans, and NOCs), People Assistant dynamically generates a signed, high-resolution PDF with your organization's digital seal and cryptographic verification QR code, delivering it directly into the WhatsApp or Teams chat.
