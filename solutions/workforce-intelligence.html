Card systems don't tell you the truth

Badge-in, badge-out systems are trivially gamed — buddy punching, tailgating through open doors, cards left at a desk. Security teams are left reviewing hours of CCTV footage after the fact, with no real-time visibility into who is actually on site.

Workforce Intelligence replaces guesswork with ground-truth, computer-vision-based presence verification — giving facility and HR teams a live, accurate picture of attendance across every floor and every entrance.

Workforce Intelligence FAQ

Do we need new cameras or hardware?

In most cases, no. Workforce Intelligence is built to work with your existing CCTV and access-control infrastructure wherever possible.

How is employee privacy handled?

The system is configured to verify presence and flag anomalies, not for general surveillance, and is deployed in line with your organization's data and privacy policies.

Can it integrate with our HR and payroll systems?

Yes. Verified attendance data can be integrated directly into your existing HR, payroll, and compliance workflows.

How long does deployment take?

Typical deployments — connecting to existing systems, configuring your environment, and training your team — take 8–16 weeks depending on scope.
