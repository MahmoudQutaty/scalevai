Inventory shrinkage and out-of-stock shelves silently cost retailers up to 3.5% of annual revenue.

In premier shopping destinations across the UAE and GCC - from flagship mall boutiques to bustling hypermarkets - store operators face a dual threat: undetected shoplifting and cashier sweethearting at one end, and empty shelves that cause frustrated shoppers to walk away at the other.

Traditional loss prevention depends on overworked security guards staring at dozens of camera monitors, while shelf replenishment relies on periodic, manual store walks. ScaleVAI Retail Intelligence turns standard overhead CCTV feeds into active, real-time perception sensors that alert managers to theft patterns, stockouts, and planogram violations instantly.

How it works

From existing cameras to proactive store intervention in milliseconds.

Edge computer vision connects to your store's security cameras, cross-references live video against POS scan events, and alerts floor staff in real time.

Connect

Integrates directly with existing RTSP security cameras across aisles, checkout counters, and stockrooms with no hardware overhaul.

Detect

Autonomous models track merchandise interaction, item removals, bulk sweeps, and shelf void gaps in real time.

Correlate

Cross-references physical cart and counter items against scanned POS receipts to immediately flag sweethearting and missed scans.

Notify

Dispatches discrete mobile and radio alerts to floor staff and loss prevention officers within seconds of an anomaly.

Retail Intelligence FAQ

Do we need to install new cameras or hardware?

No. Retail Intelligence works directly with standard RTSP and ONVIF-compliant security cameras already installed in your ceiling. A compact on-premise edge AI appliance or secure cloud gateway connects your existing feeds in hours without disruption to store operations.

Does the system use facial recognition or store customer biometric data?

No. Retail Intelligence is strictly privacy-by-design and complies fully with UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 regarding Personal Data Protection. The system uses anonymized human pose estimation and object detection bounding boxes. It does not perform facial recognition, track biometric identity, or store personal customer profiles.

How fast are alerts sent to floor staff?

Alerts are dispatched within 2 to 4 seconds of detection. Managers and security personnel receive notifications via our mobile application, Apple Watch / WearOS smartwatches, or discreet audio prompts over team radio headsets, including the exact aisle, shelf zone, and a 5-second video snippet.

Can it integrate with our POS and ERP inventory software?

Yes. Retail Intelligence integrates with major retail ERP and POS solutions including Oracle Retail, SAP S/4HANA Retail, Microsoft Dynamics 365 Commerce, LS Retail, NCR, and Shopify POS via real-time transaction event streams and REST webhooks.
