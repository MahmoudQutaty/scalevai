Talent Intelligence FAQ

How does Talent Intelligence integrate with our ATS?

Talent Intelligence connects via secure REST APIs or native webhooks to your ATS (Workday, SuccessFactors, Greenhouse, Lever, etc.). Inbound applications are scored automatically and scores appear directly inside your recruiter dashboard.

Is candidate data kept confidential and secure?

Yes. Data processing adheres strictly to the UAE Personal Data Protection Law and GDPR standards. Applicant information is processed within isolated enterprise instances and is never used to train external public models.

Does it handle Arabic resumes accurately?

Yes. The platform provides full native Arabic and bilingual parsing, understanding regional Gulf qualifications, ministry credentials, and local employment terms.

Does the AI make final hiring decisions?

No. Talent Intelligence is an augmentative decision-support engine. It prioritizes and surfaces top candidates with transparent justifications, while leaving 100% of interview, hiring, and rejection decisions to your human recruitment team.
